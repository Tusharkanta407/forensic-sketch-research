# Problem Statement: AI-Assisted Forensic Sketch Generation

## Context & Background
In criminal investigations, witness recollection is often the starting point. When no camera footage is available, investigators must rely on the witness's mental image of the suspect. Traditionally, a forensic artist conducts interviews, drawing and revising details until a sketch is finalized.

## Key Challenges
1. **Time-consuming Process:** A single sketch can take hours or days of drawing and feedback sessions.
2. **Scarcity of Talented Artists:** Trained forensic sketch artists are not always available at local stations.
3. **Human Error and Subjectivity:** The drawing style of the artist and communication gaps between the artist and the witness can lead to inaccuracies.
4. **Lack of Scalability:** When multiple critical incidents occur, the bottleneck of artist availability delays the investigation.

## The Solution
We propose an AI-assisted pipeline that translates witness natural language descriptions into structured facial features, generates realistic faces, renders them as sketches, and allows the investigator and witness to iteratively refine the output.

This system accelerates the timeline, standardizes features, and ensures a solid initial sketch is available in minutes.\n
