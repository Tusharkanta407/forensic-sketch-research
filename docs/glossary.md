# Glossary of Research Terms

* **GAN (Generative Adversarial Network):** A framework of two networks (Generator and Discriminator) competing in a zero-sum game.
* **Latent Space:** An embedding space where high-dimensional data is represented in lower-dimensional vectors.
* **ArcFace:** Additive Angular Margin Loss for deep face recognition.
* **Neural Style Transfer (NST):** An optimization technique used to take three images, a content image, a style reference image, and input image, and blend them.
* **FID (Fréchet Inception Distance):** A metric used to assess the quality of images created by a generative model.
* **CUFS:** CUHK Face Sketch database.\n
