# Forensic Face Sketch Datasets

To train and test our Sketch Rendering (Module 4) and Face Verification (Module 5), we utilize specialized paired datasets:

## 1. CUHK Face Sketch Database (CUFS)
* **Size:** 606 faces.
* **Content:** Students from Chinese University of Hong Kong. For each face, there is a camera photo and a hand-drawn sketch by a forensic artist.
* **Purpose:** Evaluating sketch style transfer quality and verification models.

## 2. CUHK Face Sketch Evaluation Database (CUFSF)
* **Size:** 944 faces.
* **Content:** Forensic style sketches of subjects with significant lighting differences.
* **Purpose:** Advanced testing of identity preservation metrics.\n
